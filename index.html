How the cost is worked out

Material = weight × price per kg, plus a waste allowance. Electricity = average power × hours × tariff. Wear = printer price divided by its lifetime hours, plus maintenance per hour. Labor = your time × your rate. The suggested price is set so that after marketplace fees you keep your target margin of the selling price.

Weight and time are read from the slicer's own metadata (Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer 3MF and G-code, PrusaSlicer, Cura). Multiple filaments are summed. If a file has no weight, it is estimated from filament length and the result says so.

FAQ

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is read by JavaScript in your browser and never sent over the network. There is no backend. We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and no fingerprinting, and your files are never part of that.

Which files work?

Sliced files only: G-code from PrusaSlicer (including binary .bgcode), OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio and Cura, sliced .3mf files from Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer (slice, then save the project or export the plate sliced file), and UltiMaker .ufp packages. Plain 3D models and unsliced project files have no print time or filament use yet, so slice them first. If yours is not recognised, enter weight and time by hand.

What should I put for printer power?

Average draw while printing, not the power supply rating. Manufacturer measurements for PLA: about 80 W for Prusa MK-series and Bambu A1 mini, 95 to 110 W for Bambu A1, P1P, P1S and X1C, and about 200 W for Bambu H2D and P2S. ABS and PC usually draw 1.3 to 2 times as much. A plug-in energy meter gives the true number for your printer.

How should I set the margin and marketplace fee?

Enter your platform's percentage fee and its fixed fee per order, then the share of the price you want to keep as profit. The calculator takes fees and profit out of the price instead of adding them on top, so you really keep that share. Cost plus a 30% markup would leave only about 13% after a 6.5% + 0.25 fee in the pricing guide's 7-hour example. See how much to charge for a 3D print for the worked example.

Where are my settings stored?

In your browser's localStorage on this device. Use the reset button to clear them.
